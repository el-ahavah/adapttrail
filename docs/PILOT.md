# AdaptTrail small pilot guide

Purpose: learn whether people can understand and complete the current journey. This pilot does not validate agronomic advice or measure climate impact.

Invite a small initial group, such as 5–8 farmers, community organisers or urban practitioners. Include people with different phone access, internet connections and levels of digital experience. Participation is voluntary. No invitations have been sent by this change.

Ask participants to read `/how-to-use`, then try: Discover → read a source → create a private adaptation → optionally select a supported assessment and town weather → add an observation → review history → preview publication. Publishing is optional; participants can stop at preview. They should use non-sensitive trial details and make no real-world intervention solely because of the test.

Record anonymously whether each task was completed independently, completed with help, or not completed. Ask the questions on the help page. Keep a separate record of observed confusion, suggested improvements and what the participant found useful. Do not collect passwords, exact addresses, or private records. Agree where feedback will be kept and when it will be deleted. This repository is public: never commit identifiable pilot feedback.

Prioritise problems that prevent completion or cause users to misunderstand privacy, source evidence or assessment limits. Retest fixes with participants before adding more features. Report actual participant counts and findings separately from planned targets. No pilot has been conducted by this implementation, and no farmer outcomes are claimed.
